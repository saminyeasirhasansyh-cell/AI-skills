# Relevant coursework (optional resume line)

Only include a "Relevant Coursework" line when (a) Samin is early-career enough that
it adds credibility for the target role, AND (b) the JD rewards the specific
methods below. Skip it entirely for senior/experienced-hire postings where work
experience already carries the weight (see SKILL.md step 6).

## EM 211 — Lean Six Sigma (Tufts Gordon Institute, Fall 2026, Prof. Pochampally)
Use when the JD mentions: process improvement, operations excellence, quality,
Six Sigma, DMAIC, manufacturing/ops efficiency, continuous improvement, Lean.
- DMAIC methodology (Define-Measure-Analyze-Improve-Control)
- Statistical process control (SPC) charts, process capability ratios (Cp/Cpk)
- Design of Experiments (DOE) and statistical hypothesis testing
- Root-cause identification, risk response matrices, control plans
- Minitab
- Course requires a real-world Lean Six Sigma project at an organization of
  Samin's choice — worth mentioning as an active applied project once it has a
  charter/org attached, e.g. "Leading a Lean Six Sigma process-improvement
  project at [org] (DMAIC, SPC, DOE) — Tufts Gordon Institute."
- Do NOT claim the "Lean Six Sigma Black Belt" certification on a resume until
  Samin has actually executed the project and received sponsor-signed evidence
  (per syllabus, due by Aug 31, 2027, contingent on a B- or better). Until then,
  phrase it as "pursuing" or "in progress," never as a held credential.

## ENP161 — Human Factors in Product Design (Tufts, Fall 2026, Prof. Intriligator, cross-listed BME161)
Use when the JD mentions: UX research, usability testing, human factors,
user-centered design, product design, HCI, design research, universal design.
- User-centered design methodology; identification/specification/refinement of
  user requirements
- Usability test design and execution (moderated/unmoderated)
- Sensory evaluation and performance measurement techniques
- Universal design principles for diverse populations
- Semester-long group project with a real external client — worth surfacing
  once a client/topic is assigned, framed like: "Leading human-factors usability
  research for [client] as part of a Tufts client-sponsored product design
  studio."
- Ethical treatment of human research participants; project management applied
  to a live client engagement

## How to phrase "in progress" coursework
Prefer active, curiosity-signaling phrasing over a flat "Relevant Coursework:" list
when there's room — e.g. under Education:
"Coursework: Lean Six Sigma (DMAIC, SPC, DOE) · Human Factors & Usability Research"
— short, skimmable, and doubles as keyword coverage for ATS without crowding out
work experience on a one-page resume.
